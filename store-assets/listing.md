## Full description (max 4000 chars)

```
Steady is a gentle, guided 30-day exercise program for building strength and
mobility in the muscles that support your knees — at your own pace, right at
home.

Each day, Steady walks you through a short set of simple leg and lower-body
exercises with clear on-screen demonstrations, a built-in timer, rep pacing,
and rest breaks. No guesswork, no equipment beyond a chair and a wall, and
no gym required. Most sessions take about 10–15 minutes.

DESIGNED TO BE EASY TO USE
• Large text and big, high-contrast buttons
• One simple session per day — just press start and follow along
• Animated figures show exactly how each movement looks
• Gentle audio cues count your reps and rest periods
• Keeps your screen awake so your phone won't sleep mid-exercise

THREE LEVELS THAT GROW WITH YOU
• Early & Gentle — easygoing movements, mostly seated or lying down
• Building Strength — everyday-movement strength: standing, stepping, balance
• Full Strength — single-leg work, longer holds, and balance for active life
Move up or down a level any time. The program gradually adds a little more as
you go, so you're always working at a comfortable challenge.

A FRIENDLY COMPANION
Finish sessions to earn coins and level up Shelby, your turtle buddy. Dress
Shelby up with hats, shades, and fun backgrounds from the closet. A small,
cheerful nudge to keep your daily streak going.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Everything stays on your device. No account, no sign-up, no ads, and no
tracking. Steady works offline once it's loaded.

A NOTE ON SAFETY
Steady offers general fitness and mobility exercises. It is not medical
advice and is not a substitute for care from a qualified professional. If a
doctor or physical therapist has given you a specific plan, follow that first.
Work only within a range that feels comfortable, and stop any exercise that
causes sharp pain.

Build a little strength every day. Steady, gentle, and doable.
```
