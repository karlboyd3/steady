# Play Console — Data safety form (draft answers)

Steady collects and transmits **no** user data in this version. All state lives
in the browser's local storage on the user's own device.

## Data collection & sharing

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Does your app collect or share any of the required user data types? | **No** |
| Does your app collect data required by other Play policies? | No |

Because the answer to the first question is **No**, the questionnaire is
effectively complete. The notes below cover the follow-ups Play may still show.

## Details (if prompted)

- **Data collected:** None. Progress (days completed, streak, coins, buddy
  customization, settings) is stored only on the device via local storage and is
  never sent off the device.
- **Data shared with third parties:** None.
- **Is all user data encrypted in transit?** No data is transmitted, so this does
  not apply. (Note: the app itself is served over HTTPS/TLS with HSTS.)
- **Can users request data deletion?** Yes — users can delete all data at any
  time by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app. There is no
  server-side data to delete.
- **Third-party SDKs:** None. No analytics, advertising, crash-reporting, or
  attribution SDKs are included.

## Account & sign-in

- No account, no authentication, no sign-in.

## Advertising

- No ads, no ad identifiers used.

## Families / children

- General-audience fitness app. Not directed at children; collects no data from
  anyone.
